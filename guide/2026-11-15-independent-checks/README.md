## 1. Equality in Nikiforov's (20)-(21) (`classify22/`) — exact, extended to ten vertices

`eq22.py` reads graph6 lines and decides, in exact `Fraction` arithmetic, whether the points (d(u), m(u)),
m(u) = (sum of neighbour degrees)/d(u), lie on one line of negative slope -alpha/(1-alpha); that is exactly
constancy of the row sums alpha d(u) + (1-alpha) m(u) of D^-1 A_alpha D, i.e. equality in (20) and (21).

| 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 | – |

Orders 2-9 agree exactly with `classify22/census.log` (counts and degree-variety distribution), so the census
there is now confirmed by a second, independently generated complete catalogue. Order 10 is new: 599 irregular
equality graphs, six of them with five distinct degrees; none with six. Every irregular line of `eq-n*.log` is
`graph6<TAB>alpha<TAB>distinct degrees`.

Replay: `geng -cq N | python3 eq22.py > eq-nN.log` (N=10 took 4.5 minutes on one core).

## 2. Problem 30 (`problem30odd/`) — the written proof checked; finite corroboration

The guide read the proof in `problem30odd/REPORT.md` line by line (sign-pattern edge deletion; the conjugation
S A_alpha(H) S = -B(H); the strict Perron comparison of B(H) with B(K_{r,s}) via the positive eigenvector z and
inequality (6); balance; uniqueness through simplicity of the least eigenvalue of K_{p,q}) and found it correct
for all n >= 2 and 0 < alpha < 1/2. `p30_scan.py` corroborates it numerically (numpy `eigvalsh`, margin 1e-9) on
**all** graphs of order 4-9, connected or not, at alpha in {0.01, 0.1, 0.2, 0.25, 1/3, 0.4, 0.45, 0.49}:
48 order/alpha cases, zero violations, and in every case exactly one graph attains F_n(alpha), the one with
the degree sequence and edge count of K_{floor(n/2),ceil(n/2)}. Floating point corroborates, it does not prove.

Priority note (guide's search 2026-11-15: OpenAlex list of all 304 works citing Nikiforov 2017, abstracts
mentioning least/smallest eigenvalues read; Semantic Scholar abstracts of Liu-Das-Sun-Shu LAA 586 (2020) and
Wang-Wong-Tian LAA 590 (2020); full text of arXiv:2501.03806 and arXiv:2508.09770; web searches). Even n follows
from published ingredients (Wang-Wong-Tian's bipartite edge bound, or concavity of the least eigenvalue with
A_alpha = 2 alpha A_{1/2} + (1-2 alpha) A, gives -(1-2 alpha) sqrt(floor(n^2/4)), which is tight only when
n is even). For odd n no publication of the minimum or of its uniqueness was found. Not a novelty certificate.

## 3. Second largest eigenvalue at odd order (`oddlambda2/`) — floating-point corroboration

`l2_scan.py` on all graphs of order 5, 7, 9 at alpha in {0, 0.05, 0.125, 0.25, 1/3, 0.4, 0.49}: the maximum of
lambda_2(A_alpha) equals (n-3)/2 + alpha in all 21 cases, no violations; at alpha = 0 there are 6, 11, 17
maximisers, at each positive alpha exactly one, matching `oddlambda2/exact_scan.log`.

## 4. `forkmatrices/` and the codes repository

The generalized-distance family K_t join I_m was checked numerically for 1 <= t <= 8, 2 <= m <= 8 with
alpha(t,m) in [0,1): all 23 members attain equality, transmission-irregular; K_3 join I_4 and K_4 join I_3 have
rho(D) = 8 and 7 at alpha = 0. In athanor-bent-two-three-weight-codes, the five `codesdeep/` programs were
